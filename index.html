GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

A few answers before you order.

Are these cakes suitable for both dogs and cats?

Recipes should be formulated for the intended animal. Select the appropriate pet type and confirm the ingredients before ordering.

Can I personalise my pet's cake?

Yes. Use the cake builder to select a size, flavour, topping and your pet's name.

Can I request an allergy-friendly cake?

Contact the bakery before ordering. Ingredients, possible cross-contact and the suitability of a requested recipe must be confirmed.

Can I choose a delivery date?

You can provide your preferred date during checkout. Your date is subject to bakery confirmation.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery is the proposed payment method for this demo. Confirm the payment method with the bakery before placing a real order.
